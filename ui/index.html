Snap refresh schedule

When snapd may automatically refresh snaps. The schedule is the snapd refresh timer: pick the days and a time window, e.g. 02:00-06:00 (a daily window), mon-fri,09:00-17:00 (weekdays 9-17), or sat,10:00~14:00/2 (Saturdays, spread, split in 2). Separate days with ,, a wrap-around range is fri-mon, and a distinct schedule joins with ,,. Leave empty to use the snapd default (24/7, every 4 hours).
